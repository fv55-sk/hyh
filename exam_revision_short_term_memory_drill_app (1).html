The 4-Wave Asian Middle Class Model

Core Exam Topic
Wave Economies Involved Primary Growth Engine Restructured Strategy
1st Wave Japan Post-WWII Heavy Industry (Steel mills, shipbuilding, automobiles, heavy machinery) Traditional Factories
2nd Wave Asian Tigers (South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore) High-Tech Manufacturing, Export-Oriented Industrialization (EOI), Semiconductors High-Tech EOI
3rd Wave SE Asian Tigers / Cubs (Philippines, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia) Services & Foreign Currency over Heavy Factories: Call centers (BPO), money exchange (OFW remittances), light electronics assembly. YES (BPO + OFW)
4th Wave China (Urban expansion: Shenzhen, Shanghai, Beijing) Post-1978 SEZs (Special Economic Zones) under Deng Xiaoping, foreign FDI, rural to urban transition, state capitalism. YES (Communism to SEZs)

The Brandt Line (1980) Key Rule:

Proposed by Willy Brandt. Loops far south around Australia & New Zealand to include them in the Global North based on GDP per capita despite geography.

High-Yield Formulaic Takeaways

Restructuring Formula:

3rd Wave = BPOs + Money Exchange (OFWs) + Electronics Assembly (Skipped Heavy Steel/Cars).

Barber’s McWorld:

Global commercial uniform consumer culture driven by MNCs & Western capitalism vs. local traditional backlash ("Jihad").

EOI vs ISI:

EOI targets Global North buyers for export wealth; ISI tried to replace imports with local production.

Job Displacement Disadvantage:

Structural unemployment when traditional agricultural or factory workers lack skills for new service/BPO sectors.
